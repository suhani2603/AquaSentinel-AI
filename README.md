# 🌊 AquaSentinel AI

### Hydrological Safety & Early-Warning Dashboard for Dehradun

AquaSentinel AI is a web-based flood and hydrological risk monitoring application focused on **Dehradun, Uttarakhand**.

It brings together rainfall, river-level, hydrological and weather-related information into a single dashboard so that users can understand changing conditions more easily and identify situations that may require greater attention.

> ⚠️ AquaSentinel is an awareness and monitoring tool. It does not replace official warnings, emergency services, or instructions issued by local disaster-management authorities.

---

## 🚨 Why I Built This

I built AquaSentinel after seeing how quickly heavy rainfall can turn into dangerous conditions in Dehradun.

Recent rain-related incidents in the city, including the tragic death of a UPES student after a vehicle was swept away during heavy rainfall, made the problem feel much more immediate to me. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

It made me think about a simple question:

**Can we make information about rainfall, river conditions and flood risk easier to understand before a situation becomes critical?**

Dehradun can experience sudden changes in rainfall and water conditions, and weather forecasts alone do not always give people a clear picture of what is happening at a particular location.

AquaSentinel is my attempt to bring different hydrological signals together and present them in a simple, visual way.

The goal is not to claim that an application can predict every flood perfectly.

The goal is to provide **better situational awareness using available observations, historical information and official warnings.**

---

## 🎯 What AquaSentinel Does

AquaSentinel focuses specifically on **Dehradun and its surrounding hydrological conditions**.

The dashboard is designed to monitor:

- 🌧️ Rainfall conditions
- 🌊 River/water-level conditions
- ⚠️ Flood-risk indicators
- 📈 Recent water-level progression
- 💧 Runoff potential
- 📊 Rainfall progression
- 🚨 Warning and danger thresholds
- 📍 Location-specific information
- 🕐 Recent observation timestamps
- 📰 Official disaster-management guidance

Instead of showing only a weather forecast, the application attempts to provide a broader view of the conditions that can contribute to flooding.
